# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales performance using SQL and Power BI. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

The project includes data cleaning, SQL-based analysis, interactive dashboards, and business recommendations covering sales, customers, products, profitability, and regional performance.

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview

![Executive Overview](Executive%20summary.png)

Key Metrics:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin %
* Average Order Value

Insights:

* Technology category generated the highest sales revenue.
* West region contributed the highest sales.
* Consumer segment accounted for the largest share of revenue.
* Higher discounts negatively impacted profitability.

---

### Customer Analysis

![Customer Analysis](Customer%20Analysis.png)

Insights:

* Consumer segment generated the highest revenue.
* Top customers contributed a significant portion of overall sales.
* West and East regions contained the highest-value customers.
* Customer purchasing behavior showed seasonal trends.

---

### Product Analysis

![Product Analysis](Product%20Analysis.png)

Insights:

* Technology was the highest-performing category.
* Furniture generated strong sales but lower profit margins.
* High discounts reduced product profitability.
* Top-selling products contributed significantly to total revenue.
